About

I'm working at the intersection of RL training infrastructure and LLM inference optimization.

Most recently, I've been working on DAS (Distribution-Aware Speculative Decoding for RL Training), which adapts speculative decoding to the unique distribution shift that arises during on-policy RL rollouts. The work integrates with verl and vLLM, and tackles GPU workload balancing for tree-structured speculation under heterogeneous sequence lengths. I'm currently based in the Bay Area.
